ScamGuard - parent PC setup
===========================

This folder sets up the "red key" on your parent's Windows PC. Pressing the
key captures the screen and sends it to you.

BEFORE YOU START, have this ready (from the Google setup you did at home -
see appsscript/SETUP.md):

    * your Apps Script /exec URL   (looks like
      https://script.google.com/macros/s/AK.../exec)

TO INSTALL - just do this:

    1. Copy this whole folder onto the PC (a USB stick is fine), or extract
       the ZIP somewhere like the Desktop.
    2. Double-click  install.bat
    3. Click "Yes" when Windows asks for administrator rights.
    4. When it asks, paste your /exec URL and give the PC a name (e.g. Mom's PC).

That's it. install.bat copies everything to C:\ScamGuard, adds the antivirus
exclusion, sets it to start every time the PC turns on, and launches it.

TEST IT before you leave:
    - a small green  H  icon appears near the clock (that's the app running),
    - press the red key,
    - your phone gets the email and the screenshot shows in the ScamGuard app
      (the app can lag a few minutes; the email is instant).

Then tell your parent, once:
    "If anything on the computer ever worries you, just press the red key -
     I'll see it and phone you."

TROUBLE? Open  SETUP.md  in this folder. The most common issue is antivirus
blocking the script - install.bat handles Windows Defender automatically, but
a third-party antivirus (Norton, McAfee, etc.) needs C:\ScamGuard added as an
exclusion by hand.

Nothing here records in the background. The screen is captured only at the
moment the key is pressed. You can read every line of capture-and-send.ps1.
